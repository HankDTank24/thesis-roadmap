How to use this roadmap

ZoomScroll / pinch, or + −. Zoomed out shows section titles; zoom in to see cards.
PanDrag the background
Focus a sectionClick its header
Open a cardClick it. Full details and discussion notes open on the right.
OverviewF or Esc (when the panel is closed)
Search/, then Enter to jump to the first match
Edit modeE. Edit text in the panel, drag section headers to move them, add cards, sections and links.
